
# Financial Health & Early Warning System

## Project Status
Under Development

## Project Overview
The Financial Health & Early Warning System is an end-to-end financial analytics project designed to evaluate corporate financial performance, identify potential financial risks, and support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Problem
Organizations need to continuously monitor their financial performance to identify declining profitability, liquidity challenges, increasing debt, and cash flow risks before they become significant business problems.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze historical financial statements.
- Evaluate revenue, expenses, and profitability.
- Calculate key financial ratios.
- Monitor liquidity, solvency, and cash flow.
- Develop a financial health scoring system.
- Identify early warning indicators.
- Perform competitor benchmarking.
- Conduct financial stress testing.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.
